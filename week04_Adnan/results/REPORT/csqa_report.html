🧠 Key Finding — Commonsense Generalization + Pipeline Completeness

The guided pipeline achieves a +9.0 pt accuracy gain on CommonsenseQA — everyday real-world knowledge — despite the guide being fine-tuned exclusively on math (GSM8K). This is the maximum possible domain gap. The gain is the highest out-of-domain result in this evaluation series and confirms that the pipeline benefit is not domain-specific.

The standout result is vote efficiency: zero wasted votes across 100 commonsense questions. The guide produces plans that make the solver completely decisive — either the plan works and all 5 votes agree, or the question is simply too hard. This clean bimodal pattern is unique to CommonsenseQA and suggests verbal planning is especially effective at resolving commonsense ambiguity.

With five datasets evaluated across three reasoning domains, the evaluation is complete. The pipeline delivers consistent accuracy gains (+7 to +13 pts), consistent calibration improvement (22–53% ECE reduction), and consistent compute efficiency (always 30% cheaper than the upper bound).
