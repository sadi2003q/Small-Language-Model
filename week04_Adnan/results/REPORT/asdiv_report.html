Cross-Dataset Accuracy Gain

Guided pipeline consistently outperforms baseline across all three evaluated datasets

Finding: +11 percentage points over baseline at 10.5B param-passes — 30% cheaper than running the 3B model alone for all passes. The accuracy gain is consistent with SVAMP (+13 pts) and AQUA-RAT (+12 pts), confirming the pipeline is robust across difficulty levels and problem formats. Notably, wasted votes are identical (83 each), meaning guidance helps final decisions without reducing individual-vote disagreement.

Lift decreases as problem difficulty increases — difficulty boundary is visible here

ECE Improvement Across Datasets

Calibration improvement grows with dataset difficulty — strongest on ASDiv

Finding: 53.2% ECE improvement is the best calibration result in this research series. When the guided pipeline is highly confident (≥0.80), it is correct 75% of the time vs 58.7% for baseline — a meaningful 16 point difference on the same questions. The guided pipeline also reduces false-confidence failures from 19 to 12 (7 fewer cases where the model was maximally wrong while maximally certain). The High confidence bucket (0.60–0.80) is particularly well calibrated at 67.7% accuracy, close to the 70% expectation.

Gain vs Question Count per Operation Type

Finding: Multi-step problems gain the most (+16.7 pts) — exactly what the fine-tuned guide was designed for: decomposing complex chains into manageable steps. Division gains exactly 0.0 pts, suggesting the guide's decomposition plans add no signal for pure division problems the solver can already handle alone. This sets a clear operating boundary: deploy the guided pipeline on multi-step and compound problems; for simple division, the added guide cost is wasted. The consistency lift reinforces this — Division lift is 0.998×, essentially indistinguishable from 1.
