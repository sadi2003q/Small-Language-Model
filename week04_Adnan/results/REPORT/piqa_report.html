⚙️ Key Finding — Physical Reasoning Rescue & Evaluation Complete

The unguided 1.5B solver completely collapses on PIQA — selecting option A on 91.2% of questions and achieving only 53% accuracy, barely above the 50% random floor. It has no functional physical reasoning capability without guidance. The guide's verbal plan breaks this heuristic entirely, restoring near-uniform A/B selection (47/53) and lifting accuracy to 79% — a +26 pt gain, the largest in the evaluation series.

This result reveals something important beyond PIQA: verbal planning doesn't just improve accuracy — it can rescue a solver from complete task failure when the solver's default inference strategy is non-functional for the domain. The ECE improvement of 71.2% and the reduction of false confidence from 46 → 20 confirm the effect is genuine and not a sampling artefact.

With 7 datasets evaluated across 5 reasoning domains and 3 answer formats, the evaluation series is now complete. Every accuracy, calibration, and consistency metric is positive across all 7 datasets — a consistent result that holds regardless of domain, format, or difficulty.
