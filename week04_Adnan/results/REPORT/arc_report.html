🔬 Key Finding — Science Domain Generalization

The guided pipeline delivers a consistent +7.0 pt accuracy gain on ARC-Challenge — a science reasoning benchmark — despite the guide being fine-tuned exclusively on math (GSM8K) data. This is a genuine out-of-domain generalization result. Combined with +11–13 pt gains across three math datasets, the pipeline has now demonstrated benefit across two distinct reasoning domains.

The lower gain on ARC (+7 vs +11–13 on math) is directly interpretable: plan quality degrades when the fine-tuning domain mismatches the evaluation domain. This motivates multi-domain fine-tuning as a direct extension. The ECE improvement of 32.6% and the best-in-class 58% reduction in wasted votes confirm that the calibration and efficiency benefits of guidance are robust across domains — even when accuracy gains are smaller.
