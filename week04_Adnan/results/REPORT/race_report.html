📖 Key Finding — Reading Comprehension & Position Bias Suppression

The guided pipeline achieves +9.0 pts on RACE-High — English reading comprehension questions from high school examinations. The guide must now reason about text it has never seen before, identifying the relevant passage segment and the inference step needed to reach the correct answer. The fact that guidance helps confirms the pipeline benefit extends to passage-grounded text inference, a structurally new reasoning type.

The standout secondary finding is position bias suppression. The unguided baseline selects option C at 42.7% — nearly double the expected 25% for a balanced 4-choice dataset. Verbal planning reduces this to 33%, suggesting that forcing the solver to follow a structured reasoning chain breaks its tendency to rely on surface-level heuristics like answer position. This effect is independently publishable as a finding about MCQ bias in small language models.

With 6 datasets evaluated across 4 reasoning types, the generalization picture is complete. The pipeline delivers consistent gains whether the task is arithmetic, science, commonsense, or reading comprehension.
