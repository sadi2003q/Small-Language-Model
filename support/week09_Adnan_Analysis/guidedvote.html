SPGFT — How We Train the Guide
1

The base model writes its own step-by-step decomposition plans for 3,000 math problems at very low temperature (near-deterministic).

2

A 5-rule quality filter keeps only good plans: correct step count, no math calculations, concise wording, proper verbs.

3

The model fine-tunes on its own filtered plans using LoRA adapters — only ~1.2% of parameters are updated.

Statistical Analysis

Is This Real? Proving It With Numbers
Calibration — How Confident & How Correct?
Calibration Error (ECE) — Lower is Better

ECE measures how well confidence matches accuracy. Guided model is better calibrated on ALL 6 datasets.

PIQA Position Bias — Before & After Guidance

Problem: 93.4% of answers chose option A in baseline — not because it was correct, but due to positional bias. Guidance fixed this.

Statistical Confidence (McNemar Test)
Odds Ratios (Higher = Stronger Effect)

It compares paired before/after results question-by-question to prove improvements are real, not random.

Finding 01
Verbal Richness Hypothesis — Confirmed ✓
Rich, multi-step training data transfers better than matching the task topic. GSM8K-trained guides beat in-domain guides everywhere — even on the same test set they were supposed to be weaker on.
2
⚠️
Finding 02
Two New Failure Modes Discovered
Bias-Substitution: A weak guide flips one wrong bias into another. Confident Misdirection: Right operation, wrong numbers, unanimous wrong votes — the #1 error type at 37% of failures.
3
🗺️

Predictive 4-Tier Deployment Taxonomy
