Finding: The guided pipeline achieves +13 percentage points over baseline using 10.5B param-passes — 30% cheaper than the 3B-only ceiling (15B). The efficiency ratio: guided gets 6.48% accuracy per billion param-passes vs baseline's 7.33%, but the absolute accuracy gain justifies the cost. Guidance saves 67 wasted compute passes.

Finding: Guidance produces 1.52× more correct votes per question. The guided pipeline has 33 high-agreement questions (≥80% votes correct) vs only 5 for baseline — a 6.6× difference. On correctly answered questions, guided consistency is 65% vs 47% for baseline. This means guided correct answers are genuine reliable solves, not lucky majority votes.

Finding: Guided ECE of 0.0995 falls below the 0.10 publication threshold — the confidence signal is genuinely trustworthy. When the guided pipeline is highly confident, it is correct 89.2% of the time vs 45.5% for baseline. The baseline shows inverted calibration — it is actually less accurate when most confident, making its confidence signal actively misleading.
