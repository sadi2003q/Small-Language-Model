Key Finding
Multi-step is the largest category (89 / 300 questions, 29.7%) and the only one where guidance provides zero benefit. The fine-tuned guide's plans work perfectly for single-operation arithmetic. Multi-step chains require decomposition the guide was not trained for — this is the most important limitation in ASDiv.
